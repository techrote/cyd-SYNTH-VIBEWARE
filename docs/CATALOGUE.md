# Instrument catalogue and creative direction

All concepts below are planned, not implemented at bootstrap. Live issues and qualification evidence determine status. Prioritize character, immediate playability, coherent audiovisual causality and visually distinct interfaces rather than superficial effect counts.

## Founding instrument specifications

**PHASESPACE:** X frequency/pitch, Y coupling/morph; second mode detune/feedback; toggle scale vs continuous. Oscillators with distinct stable regimes, bounded feedback, anti-alias strategy or characterized limits, DC/headroom checks. Geometry from decimated actual synthesis state. Sustain/release and panic. Factory patches: glass, metallic, soft, detuned, sync-like, abstract. Demonstrate different audible and visual responses to the same gesture.

**CHRONOGRID:** 3–4 tracks initially, variable loop lengths/ratios, independent phases, touch-edited steps/orbits, tempo, mute, transport, pattern recall. Bounded sample-timed events, deterministic variation, actual phase/playhead render. At least six compositions from sparse to dense, with polyrhythmic and evolving examples. Internal sound required; optional future MIDI not prerequisite.

**XENOBIO:** fixed capacity organism/resource pools and deterministic timestep; movement, interactions, growth/death and energy constraints; touch injects nutrients/repels/attracts. Meaningful acoustic mappings of actual events and state, bounded voices and event rates. Scenes: sparse, dense, balanced, oscillatory, disturbed, recovery. Compare simple and more complex behavior, show intervention replay and invariants. Extinction is allowed and reset/reseed is available.

**PHOSPHOR:** reduced-resolution feedback/history, controllable decay, warp, rotation, injection and palette; fullscreen XY, freeze, clear, exit and presets. Six distinct scenes with actual rendered replay. Visual-only legitimate; optional sonification derives from actual visual-state features rather than decorative unrelated synth. Avoid duplicating Diviner Visions; prioritize original feedback structures and controls.
