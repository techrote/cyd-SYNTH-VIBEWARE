# PHASESPACE-001 — paired phase instrument (author candidate)
Touch the field to sound; X selects pitch, Y selects mutual phase-rate coupling and second-harmonic morph. Drag continuously; lifting releases. The plot contains the actual decimated paired oscillator waveforms multiplied by their audible envelope, not a separate attractor. Colors distinguish six factory patches. Nearby consecutive actual-state samples are connected with fading RGB565 segments (maximum 40 pixels per axis); larger jumps remain unconnected to avoid false long chords.

Bottom buttons 1–6: Glass, Metal, Soft, Detune, Lock, Flux. Q toggles a Dorian-like quantized scale; tap M (commits on release) switches Y to ratio detune and coupling depth; drag from M horizontally into the field to select coupling topology: left third independent, middle third A drives B, right third mutual (original default). M shows one, two or three bars respectively. Topology changes smooth both directional depths without oscillator reset; you can change topology during a playing loop; R arms gesture looping; X cancels immediately. Tap R, then draw a field gesture and release. Up to 64 XY points are retained at nominal 32 ms UI-clock intervals; the first point is immediate, long gestures retain the initial 64 points, and a gesture with fewer than two points is discarded. The loop repeats with one released control tick between repetitions, retaining continuous oscillator phase. Tap R to stop/disarm (release commits a playback stop); while looping, drag from R into the field instead to change playback. Top third = nominal 16 ms per point (2x), middle = 32 ms (1x), lower = 64 ms (0.5x); left side requests reverse, right side forward, central band preserves direction. Direction is latched at the next cycle boundary, speed applies immediately; the R indicator shows requested direction and one/two/three bars for slow/normal/fast. Windows requests a 12 ms control timer independent of its approximately 36 ms repaint cadence; actual timer/touch/display latency can stretch tempo. A new field touch takes over immediately, patch/Q/M changes can modify a playing loop, and panic/focus loss/mute clear it. The R button highlights while armed or playing and is orange while recording. Timing is UI-clock based, with no unbounded catch-up, not sample-accurate sequencing. A contact beginning on the toolbar never plays a note; a field gesture crossing the toolbar remains a field gesture. Windows mouse simulates a single resistive contact. Escape or lost capture cancels. Native audio uses four 128-sample waveOut buffers at 24 kHz mono; software mute applies to subsequent generated buffers, not already queued hardware audio. Default gain is bounded to ±7800 PCM counts; adjust system volume before audition.

## Engineering limits
LUT-interpolated sine with bounded bidirectional rate modulation, only fundamental and second harmonic explicitly mixed; bounded frequency and DC blocker; spectral alias energy and target timing not yet quantified. Fixed-size SPSC visualization ring drops new points on overflow, never blocks audio. Input lease 120 ms of generated audio; one coherent packed control per block. The serializer is named pack() to avoid the Arduino word(...) macro; native replay includes a macro collision guard. Gesture storage is fixed at 64 XY pairs, UI-owner-only, not serialized to the audio owner. Four-row display slices, no full CYD frame buffer, rendering only UI side. Native replay and author CI are not independent acceptance. Physical DAC startup/transients, touch calibration, runtime heap, DMA underruns and actual coexistence are NOT RUN. Draft/HOLD.

## M4 coupling topology
Three matched 102400-sample Flux recordings and actual RGB565-derived frames (`topology-0` mutual, `topology-1` independent, `topology-2` A drives B) generated by the normal replay executable. Same-build partition exact in each mode; independent and directed arms produce identical A coordinates for 3200 actual points while B responds in the directed arm. Legacy eight WAVs identical against locally rebuilt M3 in default topology; toolbar indicator deliberately changes frames. New topology uses two high bits of the internal atomic control word, heartbeat modulo 64; this is not a public wire or persisted format, adapters compile with the matching core. No migration to PLATFORM's optional M3 renderer yet. Added one smoothed float in Engine; no new allocations. GCC release and Clang ASan/UBSan executed locally; exact-head Windows/target and independent QA pending. High-frequency alias energy is not established by a simple FFT of output.
